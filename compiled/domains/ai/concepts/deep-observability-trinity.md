The Deep Observability Trinity in AI/ML systems refers to the technical framework comprising logs, traces, and metrics designed to make system quality measurable. Logs provide detailed records of actions, tool calls, and decisions made by an agent; traces offer end-to-end sequences mapping how tasks are executed; while metrics aggregate performance data, ensuring that efficiency, robustness, and safety can be evaluated and optimized throughout the AI model's lifecycle from prototype to production. This trinity is foundational in implementing effective observability strategies for continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD) pipelines, enabling teams to monitor and enhance AI system performance comprehensively.
