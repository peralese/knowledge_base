Harness engineering is an emerging discipline developed around 2026 to design systems for reliable and scalable AI agent operation in production environments. It emphasizes creating autonomous systems that separate ideation from verification tasks, ensuring consistent performance without continuous human intervention. This field parallels operating system development by focusing on the architecture and environment in which AI models function, integrating sub-agent structures and persistent memory for progress tracking. Harness engineering enhances efficiency while maintaining necessary human oversight to mitigate risks such as comprehension debt and cognitive surrender.
