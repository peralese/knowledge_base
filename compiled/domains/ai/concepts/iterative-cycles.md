Iterative cycles in AI refer to a process where an AI system repeatedly executes a sequence of actions: performing an action, observing the outcomes, reasoning based on these observations, and then repeating this cycle until a predefined goal is accomplished. This method contrasts with single-shot prompting or linear execution, emphasizing continuous feedback and adaptation. Iterative cycles are fundamental in loop engineering, which aims to create dynamic and responsive AI agents capable of refining their actions through ongoing evaluation and adjustment.
