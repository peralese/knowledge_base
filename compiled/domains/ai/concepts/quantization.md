Quantization is a technique used in artificial intelligence and machine learning models to reduce memory usage by decreasing the precision of numerical values. This process generally does not significantly impair a model's ability to perform tasks, such as tool calling, with minimal performance degradation observed between quantized and unquantized versions. By lowering the bit-width of parameters, quantization allows models to run efficiently even on resource-constrained devices, maintaining functionality while optimizing for space and speed.
