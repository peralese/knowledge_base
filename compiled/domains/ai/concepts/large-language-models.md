Large language models are advanced neural networks designed to understand and generate human-like text by processing vast amounts of data. Their effectiveness is determined not only by their large number of parameters but also by critical functionalities such as tool-calling capabilities, which enable them to interact with external tools for practical applications. However, the quality management of these models can degrade beyond certain token limits (around 200K-300K) due to context window constraints, and they must be carefully maintained to prevent information degradation. Innovations like Karpathy's autoresearch leverage LLMs to automate coding experiments and iterative improvements, highlighting their role in AI-driven research and development processes.
