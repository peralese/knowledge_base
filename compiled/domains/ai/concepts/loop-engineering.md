Loop engineering is a methodology in software development focused on designing automated systems that enable AI-driven tools to operate through iterative cycles. These systems prompt and manage coding agents, facilitating recursive goal achievement by iterating through actions, observations, reasoning, and repetition until a predefined objective is met, contrasting with traditional single-shot or linear approaches. This approach enhances the efficiency and capability of AI in coding tasks by allowing continuous self-improvement and adaptation within these iterative loops.
