Architectural constraints in AI/ML systems refer to predefined boundaries that regulate and limit the actions of AI agents to prevent undesired behaviors or outputs. These constraints enhance the autonomy of AI models by minimizing errors that necessitate human intervention, ensuring consistent and reliable operation within specified limits. By defining these boundaries, architectural constraints support coherent behavior across extended tasks and maintain stability in system performance over time. Essentially, they serve as critical mechanisms for guiding AI systems to adhere strictly to intended functionalities while mitigating risks associated with uncontrolled actions.
