Entropy management in the context of AI/ML systems involves systematically addressing the gradual accumulation of inconsistencies and drifts in AI-generated outputs over time. It focuses on mitigating issues such as variable naming drift, documentation misalignment, dead code, and decreasing test coverage that contribute to system degradation. By implementing well-defined architectural constraints, entropy management ensures that AI agents maintain operational consistency and reliability without necessitating constant human oversight. This process enhances the autonomy and effectiveness of AI systems by reducing errors and maintaining alignment with intended functionalities.
