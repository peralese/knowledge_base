Context management in AI/ML refers to the efficient handling and organization of information generated throughout an agent's decision-making process. It involves managing accumulated context from each iteration or step—such as code written, errors encountered, and decisions made—to prevent token overflow and maintain focus on the task at hand. Effective context management ensures that an AI agent can operate within defined limits without losing track of previous actions or redundant efforts, thus enhancing its reliability and autonomy in executing complex tasks over multiple steps. This concept is integral to both harness engineering and loop engineering, where it contributes to maintaining architectural boundaries and ensuring consistent performance across varied tasks.
