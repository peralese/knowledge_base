# Summary

The battle between the USS Monitor and the CSS Virginia (formerly USS Merrimack) on March 9, 1862, marked a pivotal moment in naval warfare. This clash at Hampton Roads demonstrated the obsolescence of wooden warships, signaling the dawn of the ironclad era. The engagement ended in a draw, but both vessels showcased the potential and limitations of their designs, influencing future naval engineering.
